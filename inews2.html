Mode Gala 2026: Atelier Muse — When Fashion Becomes Art

By iNews Desk New York City Sunday 19 July 2026

NEW YORK — THE MODE GALA, the prestigious annual fashion gala initiated by THE RUNWAY MAGAZINE, returned tonight with an artistic flourish. All proceeds from the event will be channeled to support the arts and fashion world. The gala gathered top fashion entrepreneurs, investors, A-list celebrities, supermodels, designers, creative directors, socialites, global actors and actresses, filmmakers, artists, and special guests of THE RUNWAY MAGAZINE.

Before entering the iconic venue, The Splendor Museum of Art (New York), guests queued to pose on the legendary red carpet. The event was broadcast live on THE RUNWAY MAGAZINE's YouTube channel.
